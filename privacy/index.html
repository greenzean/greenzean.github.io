Anime Tracker privacy policy

Anime Tracker is an open source app for tracking the anime you watch. The app works without an account. This page explains what is stored and where.

What stays on your device

  • Your library: the anime you track, with status, score and progress.
  • Your MyAnimeList sign-in tokens, if you connect MyAnimeList. They are kept in the device's secure storage and are never sent to our server.
  • Cached anime details and search results, kept only as long as needed and removed when they expire.

What our server stores

Only if you sign in with Google to sync your library between your devices:

  • Your Google account ID (an opaque identifier, not your email or name) and the time the account was created.
  • Your library entries: the MyAnimeList anime ID, status, update time and deletion time.
  • Hashes of your sign-in session tokens, with their expiry.
  • The name of the platform you linked (for example "MyAnimeList"), so your other devices can offer to connect it. No username or ID is stored.

The server does not store your email, your name, MyAnimeList tokens or MyAnimeList profile data. It never talks to MyAnimeList: only the app does. We do not sell or share your data and the app has no ads or analytics.

MyAnimeList data

When you connect MyAnimeList, the app reads and updates your list on your device through the official MyAnimeList API. Data from MyAnimeList is cached on your device only as long as needed and is not sent to our server. Anime Tracker is not affiliated with MyAnimeList.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings, Delete account. This removes your account, library entries, linked platforms and sessions from the server.
  • By request: write to the address below and we will remove your data within 24 hours.
  • Server backups are deleted after about 14 days. Server logs hold request metadata, not library content, and are overwritten as they rotate.
  • The copy of your library on your device stays until you remove it or uninstall the app.
